Our Services

At Sweet Crumbs Bakery, we offer baking services designed to make every celebration special. Products are freshly prepared using quality ingredients and can be personalised according to customer needs.

Custom Cake Design

Personalised cakes for birthdays, weddings, anniversaries, baby showers, graduations and other occasions. Choose flavour, colour, size, theme and decorations.

Birthday Cakes

Freshly baked birthday cakes in different flavours and sizes, with colours, messages, icing designs and themes.

Cupcake Orders

Cupcakes for individual orders and events, with different flavours, fillings, toppings and themed decorations.

Event Catering

Freshly baked treats for parties, meetings, celebrations, school functions and corporate events, including cakes, cupcakes, pastries and cookies.

Special Orders

Special baking requests and personalised orders can be discussed. Prices depend on type, size, design and quantity.

Customer Support

We assist customers with requirements, quotations, order confirmation and collection or delivery details.

How to Place an Order

  1. Choose the product or service.
  2. Decide on flavour, size, quantity and design.
  3. Contact Sweet Crumbs Bakery with your requirements.
  4. Discuss your order and receive a quotation.
  5. Confirm your order and collection or delivery details.
