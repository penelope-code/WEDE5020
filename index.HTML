Frequently Asked Questions

How do I request a cake quotation?

Complete the enquiry form with your preferred cake, size, date and design details. The bakery can then discuss pricing and availability.

Can I request a custom design?

Yes. Use the enquiry form to describe the occasion, colours, flavour and decorations you would like.

How much notice should I give?

Notice depends on the size and detail of the order. Contact the bakery as early as possible to check availability.
